## Cannibalization controls

- `/ai-erp` is the commercial/category authority page; `/resources/ai-erp` is the explanatory guide.
- `/ai-solutions` owns the Private AI service; `/resources/private-ai` owns educational questions.
- `/erp-solutions` owns Odoo implementation; `/resources/odoo-erp` owns planning education.
- `/resources/guides` owns cost/scope/timing rather than separate thin cost pages.
- Industry guides own industry-intent phrases. Do not create Houston/city pages without genuine local evidence and unique usefulness.
