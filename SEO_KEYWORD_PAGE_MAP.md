## Cannibalization controls

`/ai-erp` owns the broad commercial topic. `/resources/ai-erp` remains the practical supporting guide, not a second sales page. Industry guides own their exact industry terms. `/resources/comparisons` owns decision comparisons. No new page should be created until Search Console reveals a distinct intent that the current owner cannot satisfy.
