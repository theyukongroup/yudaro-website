- **Ranking:** requires query, location, device, date, and position data.
- **GEO citation:** requires a reproducible AI answer containing a cited Nexavoris URL.
- **Conversion:** requires an attributed event path from organic landing to consultation.

## Funnel measurement plan

Track consent-respecting events for `organic_landing`, `assessment_start`, `assessment_complete`, `registration_complete`, `roadmap_generated`, and `consultation_requested`. Store landing page, campaign/source when present, timestamp, and authenticated member association only where appropriate. Report step-to-step conversion rates by landing page; never expose private assessment responses in analytics.
