# AI ERP Competitor and SERP Gap Analysis

Observed: September 8, 2026. Search results vary by engine, location, personalization, and time; exact Google positions require a manual neutral-session test.

## Observed intent

The accessible results for broad AI ERP terms mix educational definitions, vendor/product pages, integration explainers, academic papers, and large ERP brands. This means `/ai-erp` should remain the primary commercial/category page while `/resources/ai-erp` answers informational intent. A service-only page is unlikely to cover the complete SERP without supporting explanations, evidence, and external authority.
