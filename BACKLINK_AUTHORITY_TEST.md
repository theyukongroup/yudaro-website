## Quality rules

A useful link comes from a legitimate, relevant page with editorial purpose and sends qualified readers to the best Nexavoris resource. Reject paid link farms, sitewide footer trades, bulk directories, spun guest posts, automated outreach networks, and fake media sites. Track source, target page, context, industry, follow status where observable, referral traffic, conversions, and continued availability.
