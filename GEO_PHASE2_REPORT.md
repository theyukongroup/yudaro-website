# Nexavoris Phase 2 GEO Report

Build date: 2026-09-05

## Pages Created

Thirteen focused resource routes were created: one library index, four operational pillar guides, one fair-comparison hub, one buyer guide, and six distinct industry guides. Each route has four indexable language variants, producing 52 new localized URLs.

## Pages Improved

- Private AI service: added a natural path to the educational Private AI guide.
- ERP/Odoo service: added a natural path to the practical Odoo guide.
- AI + ERP service: added a governed-integration guide link.
- Pricing: linked published prices to the cost/scope/timing buyer guide.
- Industries: each interactive story now links to its matching educational industry guide.
- Global navigation: added Resources.

## Pages Not Created Because Existing Content Was Better

Separate thin pages were not created for every FAQ. SOP search, employee training, hardware, model size, ERP modules, Odoo implementation, AI permissions, quotations, inventory analysis, pricing, and support are grouped into stronger guides or linked to existing service pages. Existing `/ai-solutions`, `/erp-solutions`, `/ai-erp`, `/equipment`, and `/pricing` remain the commercial source pages.

## Internal Links Added

- One global Resources navigation link across the site.
- Four contextual links from core service/pricing pages to resources.
- Six contextual industry-story links.
- Three or four related-content links from each detail resource.
- Breadcrumb links on every detail resource.

## Content Gaps Remaining

- Deeper, evidence-led individual comparison pages can follow after management validates current licensing/product assertions.
- Downloadable implementation checklists and migration worksheets would add utility.
- Existing industry stories still require a management decision on whether they are real anonymized accounts or representative composites.
- Resource guides intentionally avoid unsupported security, compliance, performance, and ROI claims.

## Tier 2 / Tier 3 Recommendations

1. Publish an Odoo migration and data-cleaning guide based on Nexavoris’ actual delivery checklist.
2. Publish a permission-design guide for private AI and AI + ERP.
3. Expand inventory automation and management reporting only with validated examples.
4. Add version-reviewed individual comparisons when current licensing and product facts have been checked.
5. Add case studies only when real customers approve publication.

## Issues Requiring Human Review

**HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED — Industry stories:** Confirm whether each story is a real anonymized customer account or a representative scenario, then label it accurately.

**HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED — Pricing:** Published planning prices are linked but were not changed. Management should confirm they remain current before promoting cost content.

**HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED — Security:** Statements describe control principles, not certifications. Management should approve any future claims about compliance, isolation, retention, or guarantees.

**HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED — Product comparisons:** Odoo editions, QuickBooks, ChatGPT, cloud services, licensing, and features change. Validate vendor facts before expanding the comparison hub into product-specific pages.

**HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED — Delivery timing and hardware:** The guides intentionally give ranges and cost drivers rather than commitments. Exact schedules and capacity require discovery.
