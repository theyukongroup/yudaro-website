# Active SEO/GEO execution plan

## Week 1

- Finish apex/`www` canonical consolidation at DNS and hosting edge.
- Deploy the expanded `/ai-erp` pillar and entity updates.
- Submit sitemap and inspect priority URLs in Search Console.
- Populate branded and core query baselines with real Search Console data.

## Week 2

- Optimize the existing wholesale and HVAC guides from observed queries.
- Review the AI ERP topical cluster and add only missing, distinct intent.
- Strengthen contextual internal links from solution, industry, pricing, and authority pages.
- Confirm organic assessment attribution.

## Week 3

- Review comparison pages for current accuracy and decision usefulness.
- Begin legitimate local/industry profile and educational outreach.
- Integrate verified case evidence if permission exists.
- Run the repeatable GEO prompt set.

## Week 4

- Review impressions, positions, CTR, indexing, backlinks, and funnel conversions.
- Prioritize true positions 8–30.
- Refresh snippets only for pages with real impressions.
- Choose the next month’s work from measured gaps.

## 60- and 90-day milestones

**60 days:** branded pages consistently indexed; long-tail AI ERP queries accumulating impressions; initial legitimate external mentions; all major landing pages tied to measured next steps.

**90 days:** evaluate actual competitiveness for AI ERP integration, AI ERP for small business, wholesale, and HVAC terms. Decide whether new pages are justified by query intent. Treat the broad `AI ERP` query as a long-term authority target, not a deadline promise.
