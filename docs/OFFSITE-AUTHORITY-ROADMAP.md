# Legitimate off-site authority roadmap

## First 30 days — identity and measurement

Owner verifies Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and owned LinkedIn profile. Reconcile NAP and record approved URLs before adding sameAs. Record actual impressions, qualified leads, branded/nonbranded queries and index coverage. No link purchase, fabricated endorsement or ranking guarantee.

## Days 31–60 — publish original evidence

Implementation lead selects one actual project or internal demonstration, documents versions and limitations, obtains permission, then publishes an inspectable technical article with sanitized screenshots and transcript. A real YouTube demonstration can link to its supporting architecture article. Release a tested GitHub tool only if the code and licensing are approved for public use. Seek relevant Houston business organizations and industry associations where the company genuinely participates.

## Days 61–90 — contribute where expertise fits

Contribute documented technical findings to appropriate Odoo ecosystem discussions, legitimate technology directories or guest technical articles. Consider local events, a practical workshop or a relevant podcast if a real practitioner can speak. Publish original research with methodology, sample limitations and underlying permissible data. Follow up on earned citations by improving the referenced resource, not demanding keyword-rich links.

## Evidence register

Maintain owner, profile URL, verification date, NAP, publication permission and next review date. Avoid claiming Odoo partner status, certifications, awards or affiliations without proof. Search traffic, AI citations and leads are measured outcomes; the site changes alone cannot establish improvement.
